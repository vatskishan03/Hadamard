# Hadamard matrix of order 2636

2636 = 4 · 659, with 659 prime.

## Construction

A bordered Goethals–Seidel array over the group

```
G = ℤ₄ × ℤ₄ × ℤ₄₁,   |G| = 656 = 659 + 1 − 4,
```

with the subgroup K = 2ℤ₄ × 2ℤ₄ × ℤ₄₁ of index 4, so G/K ≅ ℤ₂ × ℤ₂. Four ±1 sequences a₀, …, a₃ on G
satisfy

```
Σᵢ PAF_aᵢ(d) = −12   for d ∈ K, d ≠ 0,
Σᵢ PAF_aᵢ(d) =  +4   for d ∉ K.
```

The Goethals–Seidel array M developed over G has order 4 · 656 = 2624. It is bordered by twelve rows and columns,
exactly as for [3964](../3964):

```
H = [ X  R ]
    [ C  M ]
```

X is a 12 × 12 ±1 matrix, and the entries of R and C depend only on the coset of K.

The sequences are constant on the orbits of a group S of order 15 acting on G. It is generated by

```
σ(x, y, z) = (β(x, y), z),   τ(x, y, z) = (x, y, 10z),
```

where β(x, y) = (3y, x + 3y) is an automorphism of order 3 of ℤ₄ × ℤ₄ and 10 = 6⁸ has order 5 modulo 41. S has
54 orbits (sizes 1, 3 five times, 5 eight times and 15 forty times), and the condition reduces to 27 equations.
Unlike in the structures of 3964 and of Alpöge et al., the order-3 automorphism acts trivially on the odd part
ℤ₄₁. Over ℤ₂ × ℤ₂ that forces the structure to be empty. Over ℤ₄ × ℤ₄ it does not, because β-invariant bent
patterns on ℤ₄ × ℤ₄ meet the energy budget of each fibre.

| | a₀ | a₁ | a₂ | a₃ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| row sum | 8 | −42 | −18 | −22 |
| sums on the cosets (0,0), (0,1), (1,0), (1,1) of K | 2, 2, 2, 2 | −12, −10, −10, −10 | −6, −4, −4, −4 | −4, −6, −6, −6 |

The row sums satisfy 8² + 42² + 18² + 22² = 2636.

## Search

The certificate alone proves the result; the search programs for this order are not included in this repository.

The structure came from an exact-gate census of symmetric structures over the open orders. The census used the frames of
[METHOD.md](../../METHOD.md) and supplementary 2-groups such as ℤ₄ × ℤ₄. Each candidate was ranked by a stratified
count: the coarse compressions are counted exactly, and a lattice Gaussian is used only for the remaining classes. That
count gave about 10^15.5 solutions here and a cost of about 10^10.4 list operations per solution, against 10^19.4
(Spence) and 10^26 (Goethals–Seidel) for the cheapest structures that `search/triage.py` had found for this order.

Every exact compression gate of the structure passes, each with explicit witnesses:

- **Level 1:** the compression to ℤ₄ × ℤ₄. There are 2954 compressed vectors per sequence and 516 key tuples.
- **The compression to ℤ₄₁:** compressed sequences with values ±2, ±4, ±8, ±10, ±14, ±16.
- **The joint compression to G/K × ℤ₄₁.**

These are all the gates the structure has, because the S-invariant subgroups of G are N₂ × N₄₁ with
N₂ ∈ {0, 2ℤ₄², ℤ₄²}. The border exists for every level-1 tuple.

The search descends through the same levels:

1. a level-1 tuple;
2. a solution of the ℤ₄₁ equations;
3. a solution of the joint compressed equations;
4. a completion of the remaining signs by an exact four-list join on all 27 equations.

About 10⁴ completion problems are expected per solution, at 0.3 to 3.5 s each. The pipeline was first checked on
the analogues with ℤ₄₁ replaced by ℤ₇ and ℤ₁₃, giving matrices of orders 460 and 844. On three cores the first
solution of order 2636 came after 257 s, and eight distinct solutions were found within about 25 minutes. The
matrix here is the first.

## Status

**New.** The entry 659(4) is unresolved in Cati–Pasechnik Table 4, and no later construction was found. None of
the standard recursive constructions applies:

- Paley: 2635 = 5 · 17 · 31 and 1317 = 3 · 439 are not prime powers.
- Miyamoto: 659 ≡ 3 (mod 4).
- Yamada: 657 = 3² · 73 is not a prime power. A symmetric conference matrix of order 330 cannot exist, because 329
  is not a sum of two squares.
- Product theorems: 2636 ≡ 4 (mod 8).

Doubling gives a Hadamard matrix of order 5272 = 8 · 659, which was open as well: by Table 4 the smallest known
order of the form 2ᵐ · 659 was 16 · 659. The sources searched are listed in [NOVELTY.md](../../NOVELTY.md).
