# Order 3676

3676 = 4 · 919, with 919 prime.

## Status

Settled by Schumer on 2026-09-17 (Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22820997). The posted
matrix is skew and comes from a supplementary difference set with parameters
(919; 459, 442, 442, 442; 866), in which the last three blocks are images of one
block under a multiplier of order 3. We missed the record and stopped our own
search when it was pointed out.

## Our attempt

- Goethals–Seidel over ℤ₉₁₉.
- Sequences constant on the cosets of the multiplier subgroup ⟨58⟩ of order 17:
  55 orbits and 27 autocorrelation classes.
- Row sums (1, 35, 35, 35), with 1 + 3 · 35² = 3676.

The same subgroup supports the structure Schumer used. If the three blocks with
row sum 35 are taken as X, μX, μ²X for a multiplier μ of order 3 modulo ⟨58, −1⟩,
the first block becomes invariant under a subgroup of order 51. That leaves 19 and
55 free signs and 9 equations, and the search becomes a single join of two lists.
